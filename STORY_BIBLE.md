# WOA: Arcane Realm — Story Bible

## The premise
The Discord Arcane Realm is the community's mythic record of the wider world: sigils are earned through deeds, riddles guard old knowledge, and the Council maintains the balance between realms. The game turns that record into a living dark-fantasy adventure.

## Opening chapter: Gloamwick
The apprentice arrives in a small town under a violet, failing sky. Three riftbound attackers are assaulting Mathias, a Battle Mage of the Council of Wizards, Warlocks & Witches. Helping him is the player's first meaningful choice and the first proof that they belong in the Realm.

## Mathias and the first mystery
Mathias explains that the ward-stones have been corrupted across the realms. The Council's records, old riddles, realm trials, relics, and bounty ledgers all point to a coordinated threat. He guides the apprentice toward the Sigil Sanctuary and its three seals: Dawn, Dusk, and Heart.

## How the Discord bot's lore becomes game lore
- **Council panel and High Wizards:** the Council is the in-world keeper of realm law, history, and major decisions.
- **Arcane Realm roles and profiles:** wizard paths, ranks, XP, health, mana, and earned sigils become character progression.
- **Sigils and events:** community deeds and realm events become the historical source of sigil fragments and rewards.
- **Riddles and the Codex:** riddles protect knowledge, while the Codex records enemies, items, spells, relics, and realm history.
- **Bounties and Trials:** bounties are dangerous quests issued by the realm's keepers; Trials test a champion's skill and judgment.
- **Grand Sigil Exchange:** the in-world exchange preserves relics, spells, cosmetics, and other earned rewards. It is a lore institution, not a real-money shop.
- **PvE and PvP champions:** friendly rivalries and combat trials are different ways for characters to prove themselves; the main story remains focused on exploration, cooperation, and the mystery behind the failing wards.

## Character model roles
- **Player Wizard:** assets/models/characters/fantasy wizard 3d model.glb
- **Optional player character, Wizardrobe:** assets/models/characters/wizard robe 3d model.glb
- **Mathias / Battle Mage:** the game first checks for assets/models/characters/battle mage 3d model.glb. That exact file is not in the currently listed repository assets yet, so the available dark-armored knight is used as a temporary visual fallback.
- **Riftbound attackers:** the shadow-beast model is used when it imports; procedural silhouettes remain as fallback.

## Opening controls
- WASD / arrows: move
- Mouse: look
- Space: jump
- Shift: run
- E: interact and advance dialogue
- F: close-range staff strike
- Q: ranged arcane bolt
