# WOA: Arcane Realm — Story Bible

## Core premise
The player is an unexpected key to a mystery unfolding across the Arcane Realm. The world should feel ancient, magical, beautiful, dangerous, and full of secrets. Discovery should raise questions before it provides answers.

## Opening sequence
1. **Character creation:** choose a name, male or female presentation, a starting path, and compatible appearance options.
2. **Arrival:** the hero enters a small town rendered in detailed 2D pixel art.
3. **The disturbance:** Mathias, a battle mage, is already fighting hostile creatures.
4. **Player intervention:** the player helps Mathias; this is the first playable combat encounter.
5. **The revelation:** Mathias recognizes something unusual about the player's arrival and introduces the first thread of the Realm's central mystery.
6. **Explore:** the town becomes the first safe hub, with conversations, secrets, and a path into the wider world.

## Tone
Mystery, epic stakes, uncanny discoveries, warm moments between characters, and a sense that the world has history beyond the player. Avoid generic placeholder dialogue and avoid revealing the central answer too early.

## Player fantasy
The hero becomes consequential through choices and discovery, not because every character immediately treats them as a legend. The main saga is shared, with optional faction quests and meaningful branching decisions later.

## Mathias
A distinct supporting character and battle mage. He is competent, direct, and knows more than he initially reveals. His own silhouette, outfit, animations, portrait expressions, and combat effects must be authored as production art.

## Continuity rules
- Preserve the agreed WOA story and visual direction when replacing technical foundations.
- Class names, stats, spells, and progression must be reconciled with the existing Arcane Realm/Discord bot before implementation; do not silently invent incompatible live definitions.
- Tames, companions, training, faction paths, and major branching decisions are future story/gameplay systems, not reasons to delay the first polished playable opening.
